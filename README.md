## :man: About me: 

I am a people-centric tech leader, founder, builder, and practitioner focused on the future of software delivery.

Over the last decade, I've worked across Cloud, DevOps, Security, SRE, Platform Engineering, and AI Security, helping organizations design, secure, and scale modern software delivery ecosystems. Prior to founding my own company, I served in senior technical leadership roles, including Principal Engineer positions within international technology organizations, where I worked on large-scale cloud, security, and engineering initiatives.
Throughout my career, I repeatedly encountered the same challenge: organizations lacked visibility and control over their CI/CD pipelines, software supply chains, cloud environments, and increasingly AI-generated development workflows.

Before building a product, I validated this problem directly with customers and generated revenue by solving it manually through consulting, assessments, and DevSecOps transformation initiatives. The problem was real, recurring, and consistently underserved by existing solutions.

Today, I am building KvantumCI, a next-generation DevSecOps and AI Security Intelligence Platform designed to become the control plane for software delivery, software supply chains, and AI governance.

Along the way, I have:

• Been recognized as an AWS Community Builder for 6 consecutive years

I believe the next decade of devops and security will be defined changes in software delivery, software supply chains, and AI.

My mission is to help organizations innovate faster without losing security, visibility, compliance, or control.

Always interested in connecting with founders, engineering leaders, customers, strategic partners, and investors building the future of software and AI.
